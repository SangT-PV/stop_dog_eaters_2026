Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data Collection

We collect petition signatures (name, email, country) via Change.org. We do not collect personal data directly on this website. No cookies are used for tracking purposes. No analytics or advertising trackers are implemented on stopdogeaters.info.

2. Third-Party Services

This website relies on the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Change.org — Hosts our petition. Collects signer information per their privacy policy.
  • Telegram — Our community channel (@stopdogeaters). Governed by Telegram's privacy policy.

3. AI-Assisted Content

Blog articles published on this website are researched and drafted with the assistance of AI tools, then reviewed by our editorial team before publication. Content is based on publicly available Vietnamese government reports, WHO data, CDC surveillance, and news sources. No personal data is used in the content generation process.

4. Data Retention

Petition signatures and donor information are managed by their respective platforms (Change.org, Kickstarter). We do not store personal data on our own servers.

5. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries or requests to remove your information from third-party platforms, please contact us at: stop.dog.eaters.sde@gmail.com

We will direct your request to the appropriate platform or assist you in exercising your data rights.
