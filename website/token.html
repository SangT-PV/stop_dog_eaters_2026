About the Token

Why a Token?

The SDE token gives the global crypto community a direct, accessible way to contribute to the Stop Dog Eaters campaign. It is a community fundraising vehicle — transparent, traceable, and tied directly to campaign activities.

Token launch is scheduled for Week 3 of the campaign, after the petition and Kickstarter are live and gaining momentum. This sequencing ensures the token is backed by an active, credible campaign rather than launching into a vacuum.

Proceeds from token activities are allocated to the same fund pool as all other donations — subject to the same public transparency reporting.
