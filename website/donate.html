How Every Dollar Is Used

We made this commitment publicly from day one. No exceptions.

Our Pledge to Donors

100% of all funds raised through Change.org, Kickstarter, and the SDE token are used exclusively for:

  • Hiring qualified professionals to manage community communication
  • Running targeted awareness campaigns on social platforms
  • Producing research reports and campaign content
  • Platform and infrastructure costs necessary to operate the campaign

Core team members do not receive personal compensation from campaign funds. A public fund dashboard will be made available within 30 days of launch.
