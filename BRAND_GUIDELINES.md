# Stop Dog Eaters (SDE) — Brand Guidelines

## Mission Statement
Mobilizing a community to end the unregulated dog meat trade in Vietnam through data-driven advocacy and high-end brand storytelling.

---

## Brand Voice
- **Educational** — Inform with facts, not just emotion
- **Sensitive** — Respectful of culture while firm on ethics
- **Data-Driven** — Every claim backed by statistics

### Tone Rules
- Never sensationalize cruelty for shock value
- Lead with empathy, close with data
- Use active, direct language; avoid passive constructions
- Public safety angle (health risks) is as valid as animal welfare angle

---

## Key Facts (Always Cite Accurately)
- 5 million dogs killed annually in Vietnam
- 95% of Vietnamese (2021 survey) support ending the trade
- Unregulated trade — no registered slaughterhouses
- Health risks: Rabies, E. coli, Salmonella

---

## Campaign Mascot — Lucky
- **Identity:** Vietnamese Ta dog, 9 years old, purebred, "priceless treasure"
- **Role:** Campaign Ambassador — humanizes the statistical tragedy
- **Voice/Quote:** "I am the priceless treasure of my family. I speak for those who cannot."
- **Visual Direction:**
  - Domestic bond framing: protector, family member, greeting guests, playing in parks
  - Pair innocence with hard-hitting public health data
  - Never show Lucky in distress — always dignified, warmth-forward

---

## Color System

| Name         | Hex       | Usage                                              |
|--------------|-----------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Urgency Red  | `#E63946` | Stop commands, critical data, primary CTAs         |
| Safety Teal  | `#2A9D8F` | Health facts, authority status, verified metrics   |
| Deep Slate   | `#264653` | Main text, navigation, grounding elements          |
| Clean Mist   | `#F8F9FA` | Canvas background, secondary cards, whitespace     |

### Color Theory
A balance between the **Urgency of Action** (#E63946) and the **Authority of Science** (#2A9D8F), grounded by Deep Slate (#264653).

---

## Typography

### Primary Typeface — Montserrat Black
- Weight: 900
- Usage: High-impact facts, campaign headlines, statistics
- Style: All-caps or sentence case, tight tracking (`tracking-tighter`)

### Secondary Typeface — Inter
- Weights: Bold (700), Medium (500)
- Usage: Body copy, long-form education, UI labels

### Weight Hierarchy
- **Black (900):** Hero headlines, stats
- **Bold (700):** Section headers, CTAs
- **Medium (500):** Body text, captions

- Shield — Protection, safety, authority

---

## Visual Style

### Layout Principles
- Minimalist, modern — generous whitespace
- Large rounded corners (`border-radius: 2rem–4rem`)
- Grid-based layouts; prefer 2-column on desktop
- Cards use `bg-white`, `shadow-sm`, `border border-slate-100`

### Imagery Direction
- Professional campaign poster aesthetic
- Subject: loyal Vietnamese dog in domestic/warm settings
- Style: Minimalist, modern; avoid graphic cruelty imagery
- Color wash: teal and red tones preferred

### Animation & Interaction
- Subtle entrance animations: fade-in, slide-in-from-bottom
- Hover effects: `-translate-y-2`, color fills on icons
- Transitions: 300–700ms, `transition-all`

---

## Content Categories (for Social/Campaign Posts)
- `awareness` — General education about the trade
- `health` — Public safety / disease risk angle
- `action` — Petition drives, CTAs
- `story` — Lucky-led narrative posts

---

## What SDE Is NOT
- Not a shock-content campaign — no graphic slaughter imagery
- Not anti-Vietnamese culture — frame as public health & majority consensus
- Not preachy — data speaks; let the audience conclude
