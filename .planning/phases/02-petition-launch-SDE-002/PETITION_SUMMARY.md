# Petition Summary — Essential Elements

**For:** Quick reference and review by Tuan Anh (Plan 02-02)

---

## Petition Title

**Primary:**
> Vietnam: Regulate the Dog Meat Trade to Protect Public Health

**Alternatives:**
- Protect Vietnam's Families: Regulate the Unregulated Dog Meat Trade
- 95% of Vietnamese Support Action: End Unregulated Dog Meat Sales

---

## Target Audience

**Who the petition addresses:**
- Vietnamese Government
- Ministry of Health
- Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development
- Provincial and Local Authorities

---

## 3 Core Arguments

### 1. Public Health Emergency

**Key Stat:** 5 million dogs killed annually with **zero registered slaughterhouses**

**Supporting Data:**
- No health inspections, no disease screening, no food safety oversight
- Vietnam reports 70+ human rabies deaths annually, many linked to dog meat
- E. coli, Salmonella, and parasitic infections spread through contaminated meat

### 2. Democratic Mandate Ignored

**Key Stat:** 95% of Vietnamese citizens (2021 survey) support ending the trade

**Supporting Data:**
- Overwhelming majority across urban/rural, all age groups, traditional/modern households
- Government has responsibility to act when 95 out of 100 citizens agree

### 3. Zero Accountability, Zero Transparency

**Key Stat:** Zero official dog meat slaughterhouses on record

**Supporting Data:**
- No registered businesses, no supply chain tracking
- No enforcement of existing animal welfare laws
- No public data on health inspections or violations

---

## Call-to-Action (What We're Asking For)

### 1. Register and Regulate All Dog Meat Businesses
- Require business licenses for all establishments
- Mandate health inspections and food safety certifications
- Establish supply chain traceability

### 2. Enforce Existing Animal Welfare Laws
- Apply regulations consistently across all provinces
- Penalize businesses operating outside legal frameworks
- Protect companion animals from theft

### 3. Provide Transparent Public Reporting
- Publish quarterly data on registered businesses and violations
- Report enforcement actions against illegal operations
- Share public health data related to dog meat consumption
