- Change.org VN version ranks higher in Vietnamese Google searches

## Translation Approach

### ❌ **Not Literal Translation**

**Why:**
- Cultural nuances don't translate word-for-word
- "Dog meat trade" in English ≠ "thương mại thịt chó" in Vietnamese (tone differs)
- Some English phrases sound aggressive in Vietnamese literal translation
- Vietnamese prefers indirect politeness in formal requests

### ✅ **Cultural Adaptation**

**Approach:**
- Maintain core message and data
- Adjust phrasing for Vietnamese cultural norms
- Consider different title (Vietnamese cultural lens)
- Ensure tone is respectful but firm (Vietnamese communication style)

**Example — Title Adaptation:**

**English:** "Vietnam: Regulate the Dog Meat Trade to Protect Public Health"

**Literal Vietnamese (avoid):** "Việt Nam: Quản lý thương mại thịt chó để bảo vệ sức khỏe cộng đồng"

**Culturally Adapted (preferred):**
- "Bảo Vệ Gia Đình Việt Nam: Quản lý an toàn thực phẩm trong hoạt động kinh doanh thịt chó"
- (Protect Vietnamese Families: Manage food safety in dog meat business operations)

**Rationale:**
- Leads with family protection (culturally resonant)
- "An toàn thực phẩm" (food safety) less confrontational
- "Hoạt động kinh doanh" (business operations) softer than "trade"

   - Understands SEO and social media language

- Hire Vietnamese translator with NGO/advocacy experience

- Tuan Anh drafts Vietnamese version (culturally authentic)

- Combines cultural authenticity with professional polish

## Vietnamese Source Citations

### **Priority: Vietnamese Sources for Statistics**

**Why:**
- Strengthens locally-led narrative
- Harder for Vietnamese government to dismiss
- More accessible for Vietnamese petition signers
- Demonstrates respect for Vietnamese institutions

**Target Statistics with Vietnamese Sources:**

1. **"95% of Vietnamese support ending trade"**
   - **Ideal:** Vietnamese polling organization (if exists)
   - **Acceptable:** Four Paws Vietnam (local branch)
   - **Avoid:** International org with no Vietnam presence

2. **"70+ rabies deaths annually"**
   - **Ideal:** Vietnamese Ministry of Health reports
   - **Ideal:** National Institute of Hygiene and Epidemiology
   - **Acceptable:** WHO Vietnam office data

3. **"5 million dogs killed annually"**
   - **Ideal:** Vietnamese government agriculture data
   - **Acceptable:** Animals Asia (has Vietnam operations)
   - **Acceptable:** VnExpress investigative journalism

4. **"Zero registered slaughterhouses"**
   - **Ideal:** Vietnamese Ministry of Agriculture registry
   - **Ideal:** Ministry of Industry and Trade business records
   - **Acceptable:** Vietnamese investigative journalism (VnExpress, Tuổi Trẻ)

### **Citation Format (Vietnamese Version)**

**Vietnamese:**
```markdown
Theo khảo sát năm 2021, 95% người Việt Nam ủng hộ việc chấm dứt buôn bán thịt chó không được quản lý.[1]

---

**Nguồn tham khảo:**

[1] [Tên tổ chức], "[Tiêu đề báo cáo]," [Năm]. Truy cập tại: [URL]. Ngày truy cập: [Date].
```

**English:**
```markdown
According to a 2021 survey, 95% of Vietnamese citizens support ending the unregulated dog meat trade.[1]

---

**References:**

[1] [Organization Name], "[Report Title]," [Year]. Available at: [URL]. Accessed [Date].
```

## Social Media Copy Translation

### **Scope:** Short/Medium/Long variations from PETITION_SUMMARY.md

**Short (Twitter/Facebook):**
**English:** "95% of Vietnamese support ending the unregulated dog meat trade. 5 million dogs killed annually with ZERO health oversight. Demand regulation now: [link]"

**Vietnamese:** "95% người Việt ủng hộ chấm dứt buôn bán thịt chó không kiểm soát. 5 triệu chó bị giết mỗi năm với 0 giám sát sức khỏe. Yêu cầu quản lý ngay: [link]"

**Adaptation Notes:**
- "Không kiểm soát" (uncontrolled) instead of "unregulated" (softer tone)
- "Yêu cầu" (request) instead of "demand" (more polite)
- "Giám sát sức khỏe" (health supervision) for "oversight"
