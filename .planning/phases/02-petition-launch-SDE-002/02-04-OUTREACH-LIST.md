## Overview

This plan creates a structured outreach list for promoting the Change.org petition once it goes live. The list prioritizes Vietnamese communities (95% of target signers), expat groups with Vietnam connections, and animal welfare organizations with existing audiences.

**Success Metrics:**
- 50+ targets identified across 3 categories
- Contact information verified (email, social media handles)
- Priority tiers assigned (Tier 1: immediate, Tier 2: week 1, Tier 3: ongoing)
- Engagement templates ready for each channel
- Target: 1,000 signatures in first week

## Category 1: Vietnamese Communities (Priority: HIGHEST)

**Why:** 95% of Vietnamese support ending the dog meat trade. Vietnamese communities are the primary target audience and most likely to sign.

### 1A. Vietnamese Facebook Groups

**Target Size:** 10-15 groups with 10K+ members each

**Search Strategy:**
- Facebook search: "Người Việt", "Cộng đồng Việt Nam", "Thú cưng Việt Nam", "Yêu động vật"
- Keywords: "Vietnamese community", "Việt kiều", "Vietnamese expats", "Vietnam pet lovers"
- Focus: Groups with active engagement (not just large member count)

**Recommended Groups to Research:**
1. **Người Việt Tại [Country]** (Vietnamese in US, Australia, Canada, Germany, etc.)
   - Example: "Người Việt tại Úc" (Vietnamese in Australia)
   - Target: Diaspora communities who care about Vietnam issues

2. **Thú Cưng Việt Nam** (Pet lover groups)
   - Example: "Yêu Thú Cưng Việt Nam" (Love Pets Vietnam)
   - Target: Dog owners, pet advocates, animal lovers

3. **Vietnam Health & Safety Groups**
   - Example: "Sức Khỏe Gia Đình Việt Nam" (Family Health Vietnam)
   - Angle: Public health risk messaging

4. **Vietnamese Student Associations**
   - Example: "VSA [University Name]"
   - Target: Young Vietnamese globally (students, young professionals)

5. **Vietnam Expat Groups**
   - Example: "Vietnam Expats & Locals"

**Outreach Approach:**
- Post in group with Vietnamese + English bilingual message
- Lead with 95% statistic ("95% của người Việt ủng hộ...")

## Outreach List Structure

**Recommended Tool:** Google Sheets or Airtable (free, collaborative, easy to track)

**Columns:**
1. **Name** - Person/organization/group name
2. **Category** - Vietnamese Community / Expat / Animal Welfare / Media
3. **Platform** - Facebook / Instagram / TikTok / Reddit / Email / etc.
4. **Followers/Size** - Audience size (if applicable)
5. **Contact Method** - Email / DM / Post in group / etc.
6. **Contact Info** - Email address, social media handle, etc.
7. **Priority** - Tier 1 (immediate) / Tier 2 (week 1) / Tier 3 (ongoing)
8. **Status** - Not Contacted / Contacted / Responded / Shared / Declined
9. **Date Contacted** - Track when you reached out
10. **Notes** - Why this target is relevant, any specific asks, follow-up needed
11. **Results** - Did they share? How many signatures referred? Engagement metrics
