# Phase 06 Plan 06-01: Draft Kickstarter Pitch Copy Summary

**STATUS: IN PROGRESS**

**Planned scope:** Draft Kickstarter campaign pitch copy that leads with unregulated trade, public safety concerns, and the 95% community mandate. Establish funding tiers, reward structure, and transparency messaging.

## Planned Accomplishments

From ROADMAP.md:

1. **Core Pitch Copy**
   - Lead with unregulated trade (5M dogs, zero oversight)
   - Emphasize public safety (rabies, E. coli, salmonella)
   - Highlight 95% community mandate (not foreign imposition)
   - Tell Lucky's story as emotional anchor
   - Explain why funding is needed (advocacy, outreach, transparency)

2. **Funding Structure**
   - Define funding goal (realistic target based on needs)
   - Break down fund allocation (transparency from day one)
   - Create funding tiers ($5, $10, $25, $50, $100+)
   - Design rewards (digital assets, recognition, updates)
   - Explain stretch goals (if applicable)

3. **Campaign Timeline**
   - Campaign duration (30-45 days typical)
   - Milestone announcements (weekly updates)
   - Media outreach coordination
   - Social proof strategy (early backers, testimonials)

4. **Transparency Messaging**
   - Clear fund usage breakdown (what money will be used for)
   - No salaries, no overhead (100% goes to advocacy)
   - Public fund tracker (real-time visibility)
   - Link to existing transparency statement (donate.html)

5. **Brand Alignment**
   - Follow BRAND_GUIDELINES.md (locally-led, data-driven, non-aggressive)
   - Maintain tone from petition (respectful but firm)
   - Use same core stats (95%, 5M, zero oversight, rabies)
   - Feature Lucky consistently across all copy
