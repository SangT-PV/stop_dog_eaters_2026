## The Problem: Three Urgent Concerns

*(Directly from petition, adapted for Kickstarter tone)*

### 1. Public Health Emergency

Vietnam's dog meat trade operates entirely outside the country's food safety framework. Here's what that means:

- **5 million dogs killed annually** through unregulated channels
- **Zero registered dog meat slaughterhouses** in the entire country
- **No health inspections, no disease screening, no food safety oversight**

The consequences are deadly:
- **Rabies transmission:** Vietnam reports 70+ human rabies deaths annually, many linked to dog meat consumption and handling
- **Foodborne illness:** E. coli, Salmonella, and parasitic infections spread through contaminated meat
- **Zoonotic disease risk:** Unregulated slaughter creates conditions for disease spillover

This is not theoretical. Families are getting sick. Children are at risk.

### 2. Democratic Mandate Ignored

A comprehensive 2021 survey found that **95% of Vietnamese citizens support ending the dog meat trade.** This overwhelming majority includes:
- Urban and rural communities
- All age demographics
- Traditional and modern households

Yet the trade continues to operate in the shadows, unregulated and unchecked. When 95 out of 100 Vietnamese agree on something, the government has a responsibility to act.

### 3. Zero Accountability, Zero Transparency

The dog meat trade operates with complete impunity:
- **No registered businesses:** Zero official dog meat slaughterhouses on record
- **No supply chain tracking:** Unknown sources, unknown conditions
- **No enforcement:** Existing animal welfare laws ignored
- **No public data:** Zero transparency on health inspections or violations

This is not how a modern, health-conscious nation operates. Vietnamese families deserve transparency. They deserve accountability.

## Our Solution: What We're Demanding

We're calling on the Vietnamese Government, Ministry of Health, and local authorities to take three immediate actions:

### 1. Register and Regulate All Dog Meat Businesses
- Require business licenses for all establishments selling dog meat
- Mandate health inspections and food safety certifications
- Establish supply chain traceability (same as other food products)

### 2. Enforce Existing Animal Welfare Laws
- Apply current regulations consistently across all provinces
- Penalize businesses operating outside legal frameworks
- Protect companion animals from theft (a common source for the trade)

### 3. Provide Transparent Public Reporting
- Publish quarterly data on registered businesses and health violations
- Report enforcement actions taken against illegal operations
- Share public health data related to dog meat consumption

**We're not asking for a total ban. We're asking for regulation—the same standards that apply to chicken, pork, beef, and every other food product.**

## Risks & Challenges

**We believe in radical transparency. Here are the potential challenges we may face:**

### Challenge 1: Government Pushback
**Risk:** Vietnamese government may not respond positively to international pressure.
**Mitigation:** We frame this as locally-led (95% Vietnamese support). We're not foreign activists imposing values—we're amplifying Vietnamese voices.

### Challenge 2: Cultural Sensitivity
**Risk:** Campaign could be perceived as attacking Vietnamese culture.
**Mitigation:** All messaging emphasizes public health, not cultural judgment. Vietnamese team members lead all communications. Tone is respectful but firm.

### Challenge 3: Funding Allocation Delays
**Risk:** Some activities may take longer than expected (e.g., securing media placements).
**Mitigation:** We've built contingency into budget. If funds cannot be used as planned, we'll reallocate transparently and notify all backers via email update.

### Challenge 4: Petition Signature Fraud
**Risk:** Opponents may attempt to spam petition with fake signatures.
**Mitigation:** Change.org has built-in fraud detection. We also manually review suspicious patterns and work with platform to ensure integrity.
