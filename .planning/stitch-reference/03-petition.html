Official Mandate — File No. 8824

Demand an Immediate End to the Cruel and Unsafe Dog Meat Trade

Addressed To: Communist Party of Vietnam Local Food Safety Management Authorities
Sign the Petition on Change.org arrow_forward

Scale of Cruelty

Every year, millions of companion animals are trafficked across provincial borders in overcrowded crates, often stolen from families. This industrial-scale trade involves extreme suffering during transport and slaughter, operations that take place in the shadows of residential neighborhoods, away from any animal welfare oversight.

Zero Regulation — Serious Public Health Risk

The trade is a significant vector for the transmission of zoonotic diseases, most notably Rabies and Cholera. Without standardized health checks or traceability, the consumption of unregulated meat poses an unacceptable threat to the national healthcare system of Vietnam.

Locally Led — 95% of Vietnamese Support This

Contrary to external narratives, the movement to end the trade is driven by the Vietnamese people. Recent data confirms that 95% of citizens believe that consuming dog meat is not part of modern Vietnamese culture. The youth, specifically, view the trade as an outdated practice that harms the nation’s international reputation.

Our Demand

  • 01

    Enact a nationwide ban on the commercial trade, slaughter, and consumption of dogs and cats.

  • 02

    Strengthen enforcement of existing laws regarding pet theft and illegal animal transportation.

  • 03

    Mandate quarantine protocols and health certifications for all livestock to prevent zoonotic outbreaks.

  • 04

    Invest in public awareness campaigns highlighting the link between the trade and public health risks.
